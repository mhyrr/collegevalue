In Kinesiology and Exercise Science, a more selective school does not bring higher pay. Graduates of schools that turn away many applicants earn no more than graduates of schools that admit most. The usual first jobs, like fitness trainer, often call for a certification more than a degree, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and many graduates go on to physical therapy, occupational therapy, athletic training or physician assistant programs.

Since the school's name barely moves pay, the price matters more. Public universities typically cost a middle-income family about $56,000 over four years, against about $86,000 at private nonprofits, and their graduates earn slightly more. CUNY Queens College and St. John's University-New York, 2 miles apart in Queens, pay about the same, yet the typical debt at Queens College takes 2.7 months of pay to repay and at St. John's 11.4 months.
