The three highest-paying programs, at Mercy University, Pacific College and Sonoma State University, mostly enroll nurses who already hold an RN license and come back to finish a bachelor's degree, so their pay reflects years on the job that a new student won't have. Sonoma State's graduates also tend to work in the high-paying Bay Area. At the other end, Puerto Rico holds almost all of the programs that pay under half the median. Nurse pay there is the lowest of any state or territory, so those low figures reflect one regional pattern and say little about the schools themselves.
