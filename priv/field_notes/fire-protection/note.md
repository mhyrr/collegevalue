The top earnings in Fire Protection come from online programs built for firefighters who already have jobs. Southern Illinois University-Carbondale, Colorado State University-Fort Collins and Waldorf University all serve working fire professionals who are finishing a bachelor's degree, often to qualify for promotion. Their graduates' pay reflects years on the job, so a student starting right after high school should expect less. New Jersey City University's program needs no fire service experience and reports low pay, but too few of its graduates reported earnings for that figure to be trusted.
