The economics graduates who earn the most come from very selective schools where banks, consulting firms and investment firms recruit on campus: Claremont McKenna College, Duke University and Harvard University. Their pay reflects that recruiting pipeline. At a school without one, expect something closer to the typical figure. The lowest figure, at CUNY Brooklyn College, comes from too few graduates to show what a typical graduate there earns.
