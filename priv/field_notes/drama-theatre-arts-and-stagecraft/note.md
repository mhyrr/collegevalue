Theatre graduates earn about the same first year out whether they studied at a public or a private school, yet a typical private program costs a middle-income family about $97,000 over four years, against about $57,000 at a public one. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that entry stage work pays by the hour or the gig and few actors work full time, so a school's name does little to lift that early pay.

Let price decide, and start with your own state's public universities. In 29 of 57 programs, parents' Parent PLUS loans alone pass 24 months of a graduate's first-year pay. Before you commit to any school, check its net price for your family's income and choose the program that leaves you and your parents owing the least.
