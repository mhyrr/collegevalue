Accounting graduates of schools that admit most applicants earn about $47,000 in their first year, close to the about $48,000 earned at schools that admit a quarter to 60 percent. The Big Four and other CPA firms hire from many ordinary schools, so the name on the diploma moves pay less than a student might guess, according to Lehigh's accounting program. The pay rises to about $64,000 only at schools that admit a tenth to a quarter, the key schools where those firms recruit most.

Because pay barely moves, the price is the choice that matters. Two programs a mile apart in New York pay about the same, yet the typical loan takes 3.0 months of pay to repay at the public one and 11.9 months at the for-profit one. Plan for the CPA license too: it has long required a fifth year of college, though since 2026 Ohio also accepts a bachelor's plus supervised work experience, according to the Ohio Society of CPAs.
