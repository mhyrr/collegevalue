In Public Relations and Advertising, a selective school barely raises pay. Programs that admit a tenth to a quarter of applicants pay only a few thousand dollars more in the first year than the open schools where most students enroll. The Bureau of Labor Statistics says the job needs only a bachelor's and no license, so graduates of open schools compete for the same agency and in-house jobs.

Price is where these programs differ. For a middle-income family, four years at a public university typically costs about $62,000, and a private nonprofit costs about $94,000, while their graduates earn about the same. Choose the lowest-cost program you can get into, which for most families means a public university.
