Two Communications programs 11 miles apart in New York City pay their graduates about the same in the first year. At one, the typical loan takes 2.6 months of that pay to repay. At the other it takes 8.1 months, and a middle-income family pays about four times as much over four years.

Public and private nonprofit Communications programs pay their graduates the same median, but a middle-income family typically pays about $56,000 over four years at a public one and about $89,000 at a private one. The Bureau of Labor Statistics says entry jobs like public relations specialist need a bachelor's in communications or a related field and no license, so hiring is open to graduates of any school. That makes the price a family pays the number most worth comparing.
