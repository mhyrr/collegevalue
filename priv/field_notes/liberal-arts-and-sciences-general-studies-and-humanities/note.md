Where you go barely changes what a Liberal Arts graduate earns. Graduates of schools that admit 10 to 25 percent of applicants earn about the same as those from schools that admit nearly everyone. Several of the programs that pay far more serve working adults who finish online, such as Georgetown's liberal studies degree in its School of Continuing Studies, and those graduates often bring jobs or military careers with them.

Since the school's name buys little, cost decides the deal. For a middle-income family, four years at a public university typically costs about $53,000, against about $86,000 at a private nonprofit, for nearly the same pay. Pick the lowest-cost school that offers the major, and in California, if you take Liberal Studies to teach elementary school, plan for the separate credential program that comes after the degree.
