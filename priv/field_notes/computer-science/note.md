Computer science graduates out-earn most other majors in their first year, and the highest pay goes to a handful of highly selective schools. Carnegie Mellon, Harvard, Stanford, Brown, Caltech and Cornell send many graduates to major technology and finance firms, so their pay reflects who those schools admit and where their graduates go. The two programs that pay less than half the median are both in Puerto Rico, at Inter American University's Ponce and Bayamón campuses, and they share one cause. Wages on the island run far below the mainland, and both groups of graduates are small.
