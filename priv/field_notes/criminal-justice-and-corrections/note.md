Criminal Justice graduates earn about the same whether their school is selective or admits most applicants. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that most police jobs require only a high school diploma and the agency's own training academy, and pay then follows experience, tenure and location. A bachelor's mostly matters later, for promotion to lieutenant or higher or for some federal agencies.

Since the school barely moves pay, the price decides the deal. Two programs 3 miles apart in Colorado Springs pay about the same, yet the typical graduate of the public one needs 6.7 months of pay to repay the loan and the typical graduate of the for-profit one needs 17.7. Public universities also typically cost a middle-income family about $55,000 over four years, against about $84,000 at private nonprofits.
