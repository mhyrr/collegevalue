In Mechanical Engineering, the school's name barely moves first-year pay. Graduates of schools that admit most applicants earn about $64,000, close to the about $70,000 earned at schools that admit under a tenth. The Bureau of Labor Statistics says employers hire entry-level mechanical engineers on a bachelor's degree with no license needed, so the degree itself opens the job market.

That makes price the bigger difference between programs. CUNY City College and Manhattan University, two accredited programs 5 miles apart in New York, pay about the same, yet City College leaves its graduates owing 2.5 months of pay and Manhattan University 5.0. A middle-income family typically pays about $29,000 for four years at City College and about $108,000 at Manhattan University.
