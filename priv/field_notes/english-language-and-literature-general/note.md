English majors earn less in their first year than graduates of the typical major. The top earners come from Georgetown University, Yale University and Colgate University, three highly selective schools. Many Georgetown English majors also double major in fields like government or STEM, so these results reflect who gets admitted as much as what the major teaches. Marist University's low figure rests on so few graduates that it says little about the program.
